Questions

Good to know.

01

Is the whole flat ours?

Yes. The entire private SOHO is yours: bedroom, living room, kitchen, bathroom and balcony.

02

How many guests?

Up to 4. A queen bed in the bedroom and a sofa bed in the living room.

03

Can I cook?

Yes. Induction cooktop, chimney, cookware, crockery and a fridge.

04

Is there parking?

Free parking on the premises. That's rare in this part of Zirakpur.

05

About 10 km, roughly 20 minutes by car depending on traffic.

06
